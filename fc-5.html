Word equations

Write and balance each chemical equation.

Practicei.

When dissolved beryllium chloride reacts with dissolved silver nitrate in water, aqueous beryllium nitrate and silver chloride powder are made.

Model answer

BeCl2(aq) + 2AgNO3(aq) → Be(NO3)2(aq) + 2AgCl(s)

How did you go?
Practiceii.

When isopropanol (C3H8O) burns in oxygen, carbon dioxide, water and heat are produced.

Model answer

2C3H8O(l) + 9O2(g) → 6CO2(g) + 8H2O(g)

How did you go?
Practiceiii.

When dissolved sodium hydroxide reacts with sulfuric acid (H2SO4), aqueous sodium sulfate, water and heat are formed.

Model answer

2NaOH(aq) + H2SO4(aq) → Na2SO4(aq) + 2H2O(l)

How did you go?
Practiceiv.

When fluorine gas contacts calcium metal at high temperatures, calcium fluoride powder is made in an exothermic reaction.

Model answer

F2(g) + Ca(s) → CaF2(s)

How did you go?
Practicev.

When sodium metal reacts with iron (II) chloride, iron metal and sodium chloride are formed.

Model answer

2Na(s) + FeCl2(s) → 2NaCl(s) + Fe(s)

How did you go?
