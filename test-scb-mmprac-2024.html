This test is about the copper extraction practical. In the original test, students could bring one double-sided A4 page of notes and a scientific calculator.

Questions (14 marks)

In this experiment, copper metal was extracted from the ore malachite, Cu2CO3(OH)2. The process began by crushing the malachite ore.

Question 1Short answer1 mark

Why was the ore crushed before it was dissolved in sulfuric acid?

Model answer / marking guide

To increase the surface area of the ore, which increases the rate of reaction between the copper ore and the sulfuric acid. (1 mark for either idea.)

Marks you would award yourself (out of 1):

Sulfuric acid (H2SO4) was added to dissolve the powdered malachite.

Question 3Short answer1 mark

Why was it necessary for the copper to be dissolved into a solution for electrolysis to occur?

Model answer / marking guide

The copper must be dissolved so that the copper ions can move during electrolysis. Electrolysis cannot occur in a solid.

Marks you would award yourself (out of 1):

The mixture was then filtered to separate the solid particles from the solution.

Question 5Short answer1 mark

Why was a rubber stopper placed between the electrodes during electrolysis?

Model answer / marking guide

To keep the electrodes apart and prevent short circuiting (or sparking), so that the current has to pass through the solution, carried by the ions. (1 mark for either idea.)

Question 6Short answer1 mark

Why was the power supply set to DC instead of AC?

Model answer / marking guide

To control where the copper is deposited: with DC, copper builds up on only one electrode (the cathode). With AC the electrodes keep swapping, so copper would build up on (and come off) both electrodes. (1 mark for either idea.)

Question 10Short answer1 mark

Despite the theoretical mass, a student was only able to extract half the expected amount of copper. Suggest one reason why less copper was extracted.

Model answer / marking guide

Any one of:

  • Not all the malachite dissolved (chunks were left on the filter paper).
  • Electrolysis was not run to completion.
  • Sulfuric acid was not added in excess, so some of the copper ore did not react.
